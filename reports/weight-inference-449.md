# #449 sibling weight inference: pricing

Generated by `node scripts/report-weight-inference.mjs`, which also writes the data the resolver prices with (`scripts/lib/inferred-weights-data.js` for the server, `assets/weight/inferred-weights.json` for the app bundle). Signed off on #449 (Lucifer, 2026-10-20) with conditions; the earlier table (#452) is the baseline for every delta below.

## Method

- **Universe.** Every product the page loads: the department caches and every catalogue file, merged and deduped the way `retailerItemsFor` does it, priced and not absurd-quarantined. **150,752 products.**
- **Resolution.** Each product goes through `resolveCartWeights` as a cart line arrives: title, store, the customer price the card shows, the catalogue weight the card carries (minus the moms placeholder stamps), and no department.
- **No-match.** No catalogue weight, and no row recognised the title.
- **Type.** The first title-noun pattern in `TYPE_RULES` the title matches, else `other`. The catch-alls are split: kit/set (gift set, tool kit, replacement pack, multi-pack, other), cover/guard (mouth guard, shin/body guard, cover, other), face mask/patch (snorkel mask, skincare mask/patch, other mask, patch).
- **Siblings.** Same department and type, under $75, with a catalogue weight or a row weight, at most 20 kg, and inside the type's physical range when it has one (`RANGES`). A sibling outside the range is a row misfire and is left out (the 1.15 kg beaded row on earrings, the full-helmet row on visors). The moms catalogue's flat 4 kg stamp is no weight at all, and its 12 / 25 kg stamps count only on a stroller / crib (on lines under $75; $75+ lines read them as on main).
- **Assigned kg.** Sibling median x 1.10 with 5+ siblings; else the department median x 1.10; else the catalogue median x 1.10 (0.35 kg). skate_snow takes sporting goods' pool for a type its own pool is too thin for. Every figure is held inside its type's range and capped at 2 kg.
- **Pinned types.** Trading cards 0.05 kg (boxes, tins and bundles 0.45); jewellery (earrings, necklaces, bracelets, charms, rings) 0.05; surf leash, traction pad and rash guard 0.3; motorcycle tyres by size, 2.5-6 kg (real weights, so above the cap).
- **Store, not bucket.** A cart line names its store. Each store maps to the department most of its products sit in, with a per-type exception where the store's products of that type mostly sit elsewhere and the exception changes the answer.
- **Misfire rows.** The rows that quoted cheap products above 20 kg carry guards (`NOT_BULKY_RE` and per-row `cheapNot` impostors in `scripts/lib/item-weight.js`); rugs are weighed by area; a lb range or a mount's load is not a weight. The guards read only lines under $75 (or unpriced), so every $75+ quote is as on main.
- **Flag.** The resolver answers `source: "inferred"`, `inferred: true`, with the basis and the cluster. It is an estimate ("peso estimado"), not a gap, so it is not held for costing more than the product (Danny's ruling on the 889).
