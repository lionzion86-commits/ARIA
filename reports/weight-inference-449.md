## Method

- **Universe.** Every product the page loads: the department caches (`DEPT_CACHE_PARTS`) and every file in `CATALOGUE_FILES`, merged and deduped the way `retailerItemsFor` does it, priced and not absurd-quarantined. **150,752 products.**
- **Resolution.** Each product goes through `resolveCartWeights` exactly as a cart line arrives: title, store, the customer price the card shows (raw + tiered margin, x the store's sales-tax factor), the catalogue weight only when the catalogue carries one, and no department.
- **No-match.** The resolver fell through to `department` (#451's band top) or `fallback` (0.6 kg generic), and the record has no published or catalogue weight.
- **Department.** The catalogue bucket the product sits in (`toys`, `sporting_goods`, `jewelry`, ...), the same key #451's sweep table used.
- **Type.** The first title-noun pattern in `TYPE_RULES` (in the script, English and Spanish) the title matches; otherwise `other`.
- **Siblings.** Every product of the same department and type with a published/catalogue weight or a weight from an estimator row. The fall-through, the 0.6 generic and the beauty "sin fila" floor do not count. Two screens: a sibling is in the same price class (under $75), and weighs at most 20 kg; a sub-$75 product the estimator puts above that is treated as a row misfire, not a sibling (listed at the end).
- **Assigned kg.** Sibling median x 1.10 (the standing 10% cushion). **Minimum pool: 5 siblings.** With fewer, or with type `other`, the product takes its department median x 1.10. Rule-matched siblings already carry their row's confidence buffer, so this errs heavy.
- **Cap.** Under $75, an inferred quote over 2 kg is not assigned; the product is listed under "Over the cap" below instead.
- **Flag.** Every assigned weight is meant to ship flagged `inferred` in the data (source `sibling` / `dept-median`), separate from rule and spec weights.
