## Doctrine applied

- **Light home only:** furniture, rugs, outdoor furniture, trees and floor lamps are cut by the store's own category. So are Pier 1's "Rewards" membership and its out-of-stock pieces.
- **5 kg cap, estimate included:** neither store publishes a weight. Each kept piece carries a weight estimated from its category: padded +10%, flagged `weightEstimated`, with `weightBasis` naming the category. A category with no defensible estimate is out (Dutch ovens, for example, are over the cap anyway).
- **Photos:** each store's own. Crate & Barrel's are its captured Scene7 URLs. Pier 1's Shopify CDN URLs were captured as `http://`; the same URL is served over https, and only the scheme is changed.
- **Prices:** Crate & Barrel's was-price is its own "reg. $X" from its price text, the low end of its regular range against the low end of its sale range. Its `on_sale` flag without a printed regular price is not treated as a sale. Pier 1 uses Shopify's compare-at.
- **Brands:** both stores sell their own label, so the card names the store once and no brand line is repeated under it. Pier 1's brand field carries the operator's supplier codes ("Gigacloudtech_DSFabricsCIS") and is not used. No brand rails.
