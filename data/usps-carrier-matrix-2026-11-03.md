# USPS Carrier Matrix — every store Aria carries

Built 2026-11-03 on Danny's directive (live call). Method: Aria's real store list — 119 retailer slugs extracted from the `*-catalog.json` files on origin/main of lionzion86-commits/ARIA, plus Foot Locker and Old Navy from the 121-store storefront registry (they run on side caches, not catalog slugs), plus Bath & Body Works (catalog lands via PR #628). Each store's classification comes from that store's own published US shipping/help policy (official page first); where the policy names no carrier, the store is marked UNKNOWN — never guessed. Sam's Club is listed for the record only: it was removed from all shopper-facing surfaces on 2026-10-28 and its catalog file is dead.

**Classes:** USPS = standard/free shipping goes USPS or reaches USPS by last-mile handoff (UPS SurePost / FedEx SmartPost / FedEx Ground Economy / DHL eCommerce) · MIXED = retailer's choice, USPS possible · NON-USPS = UPS/FedEx/courier only (UPGRADE = selectable paid tiers exist) · UNKNOWN = policy does not name the carrier.

**USPS-exposed total (groups 1+2+3) = 61 of 120 active stores.** Every exposed store gets the PDP/store/checkout alert + the +5-day widened displayed window. Group 1 stores additionally show BOTH options at Aria checkout, with the alternative's real cost passed through, and the human buyer selects the customer's chosen option at the retailer's checkout.

Notable: group (2) is ZERO — every pure-USPS store found also sells a faster paid tier. The exposure problem is mostly MIXED: the store picks the carrier and USPS is in the pool. UNKNOWN is large (47) — mostly stores whose official pages never name a carrier; UNKNOWN does not mean safe, it means unverified, and no alert applies until verified.
